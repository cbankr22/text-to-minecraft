# Attribution

The TTMC 1.0 model weights were trained on **527,084 3D models** from
[Objaverse 1.0](https://objaverse.allenai.org/) (Deitke et al., 2023), originally published on
[Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/) by their authors, with text captions from
[Cap3D](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tiange/Cap3D) (Luo et al., 2023; ODC-By 1.0).

| License | Models |
|---|---|
| [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | 525,015 |
| [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | 2,069 |

**Changes made:** every model was converted to a grid of Minecraft blocks (voxelized at 64³,
colors mapped to the nearest block, downsampled to 32³) and used only to train the model.
No original model files are distributed.

**Full list:** every model with its title, author, author profile, source link and license is
in `attribution.csv` (115 MB, too large for this repository). It is attached to the
[v1.0 release](../../releases/tag/v1.0) as `ttmc-attribution-1.0.zip` and included in the
model repository on Hugging Face. It is generated by `scripts/make_attribution.py`.

Thank you to every Sketchfab author who shared their work under an open license.
